Boat

Golden Breeze Dinghy was generated with Meshy from the Fair Winds boat reference. Codex modified the model in Blender: separated the hull, interior, rudder and tiller; reduced mesh density; rebuilt the spars and cloth; added hinge pivots and attachment anchors; and connected the moving parts to the sailing simulation.

The generation interface selected Private. The account's plan tier has not been verified. If Meshy's free-tier license applies to the generated source model, it is used with attribution under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. See Meshy's model ownership guidance and license guidance. This conditional attribution concerns the boat asset, not the entire game.

Ambient sound

Water against a wooden hull, soft sea wind, and distant harbor were generated with Stability AI's Stable Audio 3 Small SFX demo. The harbor loop has a local 100 ms crossfade edit. The original water and wind recordings are unchanged. Provider license.
